Last Updated: October 2026

🛡️ Your Privacy Matters
Python 3 Learning App ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use our mobile application. When you first open the app, you will be asked to read and accept this policy. No advertising SDK is initialized and no advertising-related data is collected until you accept.

Information We Collect

To provide a free educational experience supported by advertisements and to ensure app stability, we and our third-party partners may collect certain technical, device, and non-personal data.

1. Data collected automatically by third-party SDKs (only after you accept this policy):

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Advertising IDs (such as Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and device identifiers used for ad delivery, frequency capping, and analytics.
  • Location Data: Approximate location derived from your IP address or network data to serve relevant regional ads.
  • App Activity & Interactions: Interactions with ads (clicks, impressions) and app usage statistics.
  • App Info & Performance Diagnostics: Crash logs, performance metrics, and network status data.

2. What We DO NOT Collect:

  • ❌ Personal identification information (full name, address, email address, or phone number).
  • ❌ Financial or payment details.
  • ❌ Sensitive personal data or health information.
  • ❌ We do not require registration or sign-in, and we do not access your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Local Data Storage

Your learning progress, quiz scores, achievements, daily streaks, and theme preferences are stored locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Third-Party SDKs

Our app integrates the following third-party SDKs. They collect data under their own privacy policies:

SDKProviderPurposeData involvedPrivacy policy
Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) Google LLC Displaying advertisements Advertising ID, IP address, device information, ad interaction data Link

You can manage Google ad settings at Google Ads Settings. Our app requests non-personalized ads only.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect any data processed by the application. Data transferred between the app and third-party services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/SSL.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (under 14 in mainland China). If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights & Controls

  • You may decline this policy; in that case the app will close and no data will be collected.
  • You may reset your progress from the app settings, or clear the app data in your device settings.
  • You may reset or limit your Advertising ID in your device system settings.
  • You may uninstall the app at any time.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

1. 第三方 SDK 自动收集的数据（仅在您同意本政策后）：

  • 应用活动与交互：与广告的交互（点击、展示）以及应用使用统计。
  • 应用信息与性能诊断：崩溃日志、性能指标和网络状态数据。

2. 我们不收集的信息：

  • ❌ 个人身份信息（姓名、住址、电子邮箱或手机号码）。
