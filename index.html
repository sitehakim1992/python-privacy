🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

🛡️️ Your Privacy Matters
Python 3 Learning App ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

To provide a free educational experience supported by advertisements and to ensure app stability, we and our trusted third-party partners (such as advertising and analytics networks) may collect certain technical, device, and non-personal data.

1. Data Collected Automatically (via Third-Party SDKs like AdMob & Start.io):

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Advertising IDs (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID), IP addresses, and unique device identifiers used for ad personalization, frequency capping, attribution, and analytics.
  • Location Data: Coarse or approximate location derived from your IP address or network data to serve relevant regional content and ads.
  • App Activity & Interactions: Interactions with ads (clicks, impressions, views), app usage statistics, and navigation patterns to evaluate ad effectiveness and improve user experience.
  • App Info & Performance Diagnostics: Crash logs, performance metrics, and network status data used to diagnose issues and optimize performance.

2. What We DO NOT Collect:

  • ❌ Personal identification information (such as your full name, physical address, direct email address, or phone number).
  • ❌ Financial or payment details.
  • ❌ Sensitive personal data or health information.

Local Data Storage

Your learning progress, quiz scores, achievements, daily streaks, and theme preferences are stored locally on your device only. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to or stored on our external servers.

Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

Our app integrates third-party SDKs to display advertisements and collect analytics. These partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and technical data in accordance with their respective privacy policies to serve tailored ads and measure performance.

Our primary third-party advertising partners include:

  • Google AdMob: Provided by Google LLC. Google uses device identifiers and cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can learn more about how Google handles user data by reading the Google Privacy & Terms and manage your ad settings via Google Ads Settings.
  • Start.io: Provided by Start.io Inc. You can learn more about their data practices and opt-out options by visiting the Start.io Privacy Policy.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect any data processed by the application. All data transferred between the app and third-party services is encrypted in transit using secure network protocols (HTTPS/SSL).

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. We comply with applicable regulations including COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR. If you believe that a child has provided personal data improperly, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures to delete it.

Data Deletion & User Controls

You can clear your local progress data and reset advertising choices at any time by:

  • Using the reset progress options inside the app settings.
  • Resetting or limiting your Advertising ID in your device's Android/iOS system settings.
  • Clearing the application data through your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the application entirely from your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
